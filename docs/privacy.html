Virtue Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

🛡️ Zero Data Collection: Virtue is built on a zero-knowledge architecture. We do not own servers, we do not collect your personal data, and we will never track, sell, or monetize your browsing history.

1. Overview

Virtue ("we", "our", or "the extension") is an open-source browser extension designed to provide transparent web accountability and telemetry for individuals and trusted partners. Your privacy is protected by design: all logged activity remains strictly under your ownership inside your personal Google Account.

2. Information Processed by the Extension

To provide accountability features, Virtue processes the following information locally on your device:

  • Browsing Telemetry: Domain names, URL paths, page titles, search queries, and access timestamps.
  • Security & Diagnostics: Heartbeat timestamps, device labels (e.g., Windows PC, Mac), and InPrivate/Incognito monitoring state.
  • Accountability Settings: Designated accountability partner email addresses, custom blocked site lists, and user PIN hashes.

3. How Your Data Is Stored & Encrypted

Virtue does not transmit your data to any central server or developer database. Storage and encryption are handled as follows:

  • Local Storage: Temporary browsing logs and user configurations are stored locally in your browser’s isolated storage area (chrome.storage.local).
  • Client-Side Encryption: Log data is encrypted locally using client-side AES-GCM encryption before being transmitted anywhere.
  • Google Drive Storage: Encrypted activity logs are synced directly to a dedicated file in your personal Google Drive account. Only you and individuals holding your unique decryption key link can decrypt and view the logs.

4. How Permissions & Google Services Are Used

Virtue requests minimal, targeted permissions required for core accountability functionality:

  • drive.file: Grants access strictly to the single log file created by Virtue in your Google Drive. Virtue cannot read, edit, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.
  • gmail.send: Used exclusively to dispatch automated accountability digest emails and security alerts directly from your Google Account to your designated partner.
  • webNavigation & declarativeNetRequest: Used to detect unmonitored browsing gaps, apply dynamic filtering rules, and direct restricted URLs to the warning block page.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not share, transfer, or sell your personal data to any third party under any circumstances. There are no analytics trackers, advertising networks, or external telemetry services integrated into Virtue.

6. User Control & Data Retention

  • Rolling Data Retention: Virtue automatically maintains a rolling 7-day retention window for activity logs. Older entries are pruned automatically to minimize storage footprint.
  • Data Deletion: You can purge all stored activity at any time by disconnecting your Google Account from the extension popup, uninstalling the extension, or deleting the virtue_logs.json file directly from your Google Drive.

7. Contact & Support

Virtue is an open-source project. If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or security practices, you can inspect the source code or submit an issue on our public repository at GitHub.
